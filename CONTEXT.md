# Pittogramma

Pittogramma is a cultural platform covering graphic design, visual culture, events, journal content, and related resources.

## Language

**Upcoming (events)**:
Events that have not yet ended. Includes events scheduled for the future and events currently in progress.
_Avoid_: Next, future (in user-facing copy)

**Past (events)**:
Events that have ended.

**Event status**:
Operational state of an upcoming event (e.g. tickets available, sold out, cancelled, postponed). Status affects badges and CTAs but does not move an event out of Upcoming before its end date — cancelled and postponed events remain visible until they end.
