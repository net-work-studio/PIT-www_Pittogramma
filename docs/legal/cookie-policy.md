## What cookies and similar storage are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store in your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can store information in your browser without using a cookie.

This policy covers both cookies and similar browser storage. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) explains how we process personal data more generally.

## Storage used on the public website

Pittogramma does not currently use advertising, behavioural tracking, analytics, maps, or external video embeds on the public website.

The website stores your colour-theme preference in your browser's local storage. This is technical storage. It remembers whether you selected the light, dark, or system theme and is not used to identify you, measure your activity, or show advertising.

Because the public website uses only this technical storage, it does not show a site-wide cookie banner or preference centre.

Our hosting and content-delivery providers may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information, and security logs when they deliver the website. This information is not stored by Pittogramma as a browser cookie. See the Privacy Policy for details.

## Project submissions and payments

The `/submit` page embeds a form provided by Fillout. Fillout may use cookies or similar storage to operate the form, remember an incomplete response, provide security, and support payment. Payment is processed by Stripe through the Fillout form. Fillout and Stripe handle their own browser storage under their respective notices.

If Fillout asks for consent to optional cookies or similar technology, you can make that choice in the control shown in the form. You can read Fillout's [privacy and GDPR information](https://www.fillout.com/help/gdpr) and Stripe's [Privacy Center](https://stripe.com/privacy).

You can avoid this third-party storage by not using the submission form. This may mean that you cannot submit a project through the website.

## How to manage storage

You can remove or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Doing so may reset your theme preference or prevent the submission form from working as intended.

We may update this policy when the website, its services, or the law changes. The current version will be published here with a new "Last updated" date. If we add non-essential cookies or tracking, we will put an appropriate consent process in place before using them where the law requires it.
